# Pick row

One of the five picks on This week.

- Meta line in `small` `muted`, the slot and the source joined by a middle dot, then the tag.
- Title in `pick`, up to three lines.
- A chevron on the right. When read, the title turns `muted` and the chevron becomes the `primary` check.
- Rows are split by a `line` hairline, 14 above and below.
